Introduction

Journey In The Sky is a Dancing Line Fanmade Game, developed by JITS Studio. Explore every level of this game, follow the rhythm and avoid the obstacles, unless reaching to the end, using music.

Journey In The Sky includes 47 levels. All of them have their own story, and with each level reaches difficulty.
