Introduction

Journey In The Sky is a Dancing Line Fanmade Game, developed by JITS Studio. Explore every level of this game, follow the rhythm and avoid the obstacles, unless reaching to the end, using music.

Journey In The Sky includes 47 levels. All of them have their own story, and with each level reaches difficulty.

Histroy

Idea about making Dancing Line Fanmade Game was born at August 2022. At that time Dju de la va made 5 levels and 3 of them was published, meanwhile 2 other was not finished. Idea was simple: combine 5 ready levels in one game. From the August 2022 Dju de la va worked with future big game alone and published 3 versions. At 4th September 2022 Journey In The Sky came out. After 5th September 2022 ttt joined to the project and made 2 levels for the game in 2022. At 16th February 2023 we published our game on DL RS Community Official Website and entered in [Bilibili](https://space.bilibili.com/3493137997629710) with name JITS Studio. Since 3rd March 2023 we started to cooperate with Chinese DL Fanmade creators and first our cooperation level was Harmony by Assassin.
